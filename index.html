By Age

who will love what
✦

Toddlers & Little Ones · 2–6

  • Enchanted Island — rides built for exactly this age.
  • Medieval Times — horses, knights, and eating with your hands.
  • Musical Instrument Museum — bang on everything in the Experience Gallery.
  • Children's Museum of Phoenix — burn energy indoors.
  • Butterfly Wonderland — calm, cool, and magical.
  • Phoenix Zoo — splash pad, carousel, petting area.
  • MacDonald's Ranch — pony rides, petting zoo, pumpkin patch.
  • Desert Belle — a boat ride with no walking required.
✦

Kids · 7–12

  • Golfland Sunsplash — batting cages, mini golf, karts.
  • iFLY — indoor skydiving, no experience needed.
  • Slide Rock — a natural water slide in Sedona.
  • Arizona Science Center — hands-on everything.
  • OdySea Aquarium — touch tanks and the rotating theater.
  • Out of Africa — Tiger Splash and the safari tram.
  • Papago Park — a "real" hike they can finish.
✦

Teens · 13–19

  • Predator Zip Line at Out of Africa — over the lions.
  • Octane Raceway — 45-mph karts, VR, axe throwing.
  • Hot Air Expeditions — sunrise balloon over the desert.
  • Kayaking the Salt River — wild horses along the bank.
  • Horseback at MacDonald's Ranch — sunset desert ride.
  • Arizona Zipline Adventures — five lines over high desert near Tucson.
  • Camelback Mountain — the sunrise scramble.
  • Topgolf & Golfland — friendly competition after dark.
  • Mavericks Hat Bar — design a custom cowboy hat in Old Town.

Good To Know

a few notes before you go
✦

Pairings That Save a Drive

  • Papago Park day: Phoenix Zoo right at 8am opening → lunch and a pool break → Desert Botanical Garden and Hole-in-the-Rock at sunset. All within a mile of each other.
  • Boardwalk day: OdySea Aquarium + Butterfly Wonderland share a parking lot; Octane Raceway is 5 minutes away.
  • Lake day: Morning kayaks at Saguaro Lake Guest Ranch, lunch at the marina, afternoon Desert Belle cruise.
  • Downtown day: Children's Museum for the littles, Science Center for the older kids — across the street from each other.
  • Camp Verde day: Out of Africa in the morning, Montezuma Castle on the way back — they're ten minutes apart.
  • Sedona day: Slide Rock early (before the lot fills), Pink Jeep tour mid-afternoon, dinner in Uptown Sedona. ~2 hrs each way.
  • Old Town afternoon: Mavericks Hat Bar, then the free Old Town trolley, ice cream, and dinner on 5th Ave.
  • Talking Stick night: iFLY, Octane Raceway, Topgolf, and Medieval Times are all within a mile of each other — split the group by age and meet for dinner.
✦

Late-September Weather

Still warm — expect highs in the mid-90s, sometimes pushing 100°F, with pleasant mornings and evenings in the 70s. Do outdoor things (zoo, ranch, hikes, lake) before 11am or after 4pm, and save the aquarium, museums, and Octane for the hot middle of the day. Camp Verde runs about 10° cooler than the Valley.

Sunscreen, hats, and a water bottle for everyone, every day. Heat sneaks up on kids fast.
